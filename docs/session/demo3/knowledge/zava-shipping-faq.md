# Zava shipping FAQ

_Fictional demo content. Updated 15 September 2026._

## How long does delivery take?

| Destination | Standard | Express |
| --- | --- | --- |
| France, Germany, Benelux | 2–4 business days | Next business day (order before 14:00 CET) |
| Rest of the EU | 4–7 business days | 2 business days |
| United Kingdom, Switzerland | 5–8 business days | 3 business days (duties prepaid) |

## How much does shipping cost?

- Standard shipping is **free** for orders above **€60**; otherwise €4.90.
- Express shipping costs **€12.90**, or €6.90 for Zava Plus members.
- Outdoor furniture ships by freight: €49 flat fee, free above €500.

## Can I change my delivery address?

Yes, until the order status changes to **Packed** (usually within two hours). After that, contact support and the
carrier will try to redirect the parcel; redirection is not guaranteed.

## Do you ship outside Europe?

Not today. Orders with a delivery address outside the countries listed above are cancelled automatically and refunded
in full.
